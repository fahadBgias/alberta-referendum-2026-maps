About these numbers

Reading the map

Each shape is one of Alberta's 87 provincial electoral divisions. Its colour shows which side is ahead there: blue for Yes and red for No, and on Question 10, red for Remain and blue for Referendum. A lighter shade means the lead is under 5 percentage points. Grey means no votes have been reported yet for that question.

Map area is not population. The large rural divisions fill most of the Alberta map, while the 46 Edmonton and Calgary divisions hold roughly half the province's voters.

Where the count stands

These are Elections Alberta's unofficial results, still being hand-counted. Question 10 was counted first and Questions 1 to 9 follow in order, so the later questions have fewer votes counted and can still shift. Official results are scheduled for October 29, 2026.

This page picks up new numbers automatically. A script checks Elections Alberta's results feed every 15 minutes, and the page looks for an update every 5 minutes while it is open.
